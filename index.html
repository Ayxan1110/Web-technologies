Interesting topic: Basketball

History of the game

Basketball was invented in 1891 by a teacher called James Naismith in Springfield, Massachusetts. He wanted to make a new game for his students to play inside the gym during the cold winter, so he hanged two peach baskets on the wall and wrote 13 simple rules. In the first games the players used a football and every time somebody scored, one person had to take the ball out of the basket with a stick, because the basket had no hole in the bottom.

Today basketball is one of the most popular sports in the world and it is played by millions of people. A team has five players on the court and they try to throw the ball into the ring of the other team. A normal shot gives 2 points, a shot from far away gives 3 points and a free throw gives 1 point. The biggest competitions are the NBA in America, the EuroLeague in Europe and also the Olympic Games, where the best national teams play against each other.
